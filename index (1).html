Four filters before liftoff

A project only reaches the pad after clearing every stage. If it fails one, it doesn't fly.

  1. On-chain checks

    Automatic: no hidden minting, no wallet freezing, locked liquidity and a healthy holder spread.

  2. Crim's review

    Our AI reads the whitepaper, website, code and tokenomics, flags red flags and writes a public report.

  3. Team verification

    Real people behind every project, verified by the Crimson Orbit team.

  4. Community vote

    $CRIM holders vote on which approved projects get to launch.

Meet Crim

Crim is the AI that inspects every rocket before it's cleared for launch. It reads everything a project publishes, checks it against what's on-chain and writes a report anyone can read.

Crim helps people decide. It doesn't decide alone: every approval also goes through human verification and a community vote.

How to buy $CRIM

All you need is a Solana wallet and some SOL.

  1. Install Phantom

    Get it on your phone or browser, and write your recovery phrase down on paper. Keep it somewhere safe.

  2. Get SOL

    Buy SOL on an exchange and send it to your Phantom address.

  3. Open Jupiter

    Connect your wallet at jup.ag and paste the $CRIM contract address.

  4. Swap

    Choose how much SOL to swap, confirm in Phantom, and $CRIM lands in your wallet.
